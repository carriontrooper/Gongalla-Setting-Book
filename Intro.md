**What is Gongalla?**

It started out as the setting of two of my quests which I ran on 4chan's /qst/ board: Gaol Quest and Jail Quest.
Over time, it became a more fleshed-out setting, and I want a way for the setting to live on after the quests have finished.

**Yeah, but what *is* Gongalla?**

Gongalla Gaol is a pocket universe created by the multi-universe spanning Eternal Empire, to house their most dangerous or problematic criminals from their many realms.
Or at least, that's how it started.
Over time, it became the 'dumping grounds' of inmates and criminals once the normal prisons overflow; and it started to develop its own culture and civilizations from the prisoners and wardens stuck inside.
It got even partially abandoned, a short while before a calamity hit the core universe of the Eternal Empire...

**Cool, so what's gonna be in this?**

There's gonna be:
- the different places inside the Gongalla pocket universe
- the different entities and races inside (and sometimes outside) Gongalla
- the different factions (over different times) of Gongalla
- the different universes outside Gongalla (that we know of)
- the different powers/skills of the inhabitants of Gongalla
- a rough timeline of what events happened in Gongalla (and beyond!)
- some example campaigns that can happen in Gongalla

**Neat, but what's this warning thing about?**

Since this is a setting about a prison dimension full of criminals, with sometimes unspeakable crimes:
*Some crimes or sensibilities might be a bit trigger warning-y.*
If possible, they will be euphemized or re-thesaurus'd, and if not, viewer discretion advised, and warned about. Like, say, non-consensualization. Or the frankly messed up relationships that may happen due to timey wimey shenanigans caused by Eternal Empire dimensional tech.
But if you really do not want to include said messed up parts in your game:
**That is absolutely fine with me.**
Not everyone can, will, or want to tackle such subjects, and it is perfectly ok to excise or work around or replace the problematic bits. Just be forewarned that you might need some serious effort to rework them if you decide to do so. Or cleaning up the edges after redacting the messed up bits.

Anyways, I hope you'll have fun checking out this messed up, hodge-podge, patched together setting of mine! Maybe even run a game or two in it!
