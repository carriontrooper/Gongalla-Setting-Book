**The Locations of Gongalla Gaol**

What would one expect to see inside Gongalla?
A new inmate might notice that thankfully, there's a floor; one that extends endlessly in the four cardinal directions, if one can remove all obstacles that blocks line of sight.

Then they might look up and see a ceiling above them. Whether it's close by like right above their head, or a bit far above that you can see clouds beginning to form - there is a ceiling above every place in Gongalla (unless you're above the ceiling, but we'll get to that later).

Further on, one might see that this endless expanse of a prison can be roughly divided into four (or five) regions: North, East, South, and West (and sometimes Center).

So, there are three 'height layers' to Gongalla, that is the Floor, the Ceiling, and Underground.

But if one is intrepid enough (or unlucky enough), they can find themselves outside the reality-brane of Gongalla, standing on the wrong side of the boundary of the Is and Is-Not that surrounds the space between universes. This place between universes is called the **Nowhere**.
