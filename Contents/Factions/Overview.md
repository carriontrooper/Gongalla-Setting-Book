**The various factions of Gongalla Gaol**

Whenever there are a numerous and diverse lot of people gathered together in one place, such as one pocket universe's worth, of course groups and factions will form among them.

As such, here are some notable factions you can use in your games. Not to mention the less notable ones, which you can invent wholesale! Which is definitely easier to do in this kitchen sink of a setting.
