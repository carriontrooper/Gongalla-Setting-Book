**The denizens of Gongalla Gaol**

Since its creation at the hands of Eternal Empire, Gongalla has been host to numerous sapient races - most of them originating as prisoners of the Empire.
Some, however, are their wardens; who, unbeknownst to them, are also stuck in Gongalla with the rest of them.
But at least one sapient race is *native* to Gongalla, which proves that life finds a way - even inside a pocket universe.
Then there's the startlingly few non-sapient ones, invasive species that found their way inside.
And then there are the deities...
